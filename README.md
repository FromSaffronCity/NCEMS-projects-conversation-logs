# conversation-logs

Conversation and experimental logs from my NCEMS internship work, collaborating with a Claude agent.

Project: read-backed haplotype phasing of heterozygous SNPs called from single-cell **bisulfite**
sequencing of human brain (snMC-seq and snM3C-seq), across three donors and several pseudo-bulk
depths. 2026-07-12 → present.
